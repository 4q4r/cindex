## Design Context

### Users

Researchers, graduate students, and article authors who need to quickly find citable scholarly sources.
The product is used as a focused research tool, not as a marketing site.

### Brand Personality

Academic, restrained, trustworthy.
The interface should feel precise and quiet, with confident information hierarchy.

### Aesthetic Direction

Minimalist dark theme.
Search-engine-first structure (query and filtering as primary surface, results as dominant content area).
Avoid landing-page patterns and avoid cramped block spacing.

### Design Principles

1. Search workflow first: query and filters are always the primary interaction.
2. Calm density: enough whitespace between surfaces, no block crowding.
3. Evidence-forward UI: metadata, identifiers, and eligibility evidence are clear and scannable.
4. Minimal visual noise: restrained accents, no decorative gimmicks.
5. Practical readability: long snippets remain readable and copy actions are obvious.
